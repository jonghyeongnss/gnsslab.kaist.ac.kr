Jiyun Lee

KAIST Endowed Chair Professor · Department of Aerospace Engineering

Prof. Jiyun Lee leads the GNSS Lab at KAIST, where her research focuses on GNSS integrity monitoring, satellite-based augmentation systems, ionospheric modeling for Global Navigation Satellite System applications, and GNSS radio occultation for atmospheric remote sensing. She has made foundational contributions to ground-based and satellite-based augmentation system (GBAS/SBAS) integrity standards and has pioneered the use of CubeSat platforms for ionospheric and space weather monitoring.
